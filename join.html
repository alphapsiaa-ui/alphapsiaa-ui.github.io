Stay Connected

Once a brother, always a brother

The Alpha Psi Alumni Association (AΨAA) gives brothers of Kappa Kappa Psi a way to stay connected, continue serving, support the active chapter, and build relationships with fellow Alpha Psi alumni.

Why Join?

Three reasons to come home

Brotherhood

Reconnect with old friends, meet fellow Alpha Psi alumni, and continue building the brotherhood that began during your time at WTAMU.

Service

Keep living out the values of Kappa Kappa Psi by giving back to Alpha Psi, the WTAMU band programs, and our surrounding communities.

Support

Help provide resources, opportunities, scholarships, grants, and programs that support our active chapter and fellow alumni.

Who Can Join?

Eligibility

The Alpha Psi Alumni Association is exclusively for Alumni, Life, and Honorary members of the Alpha Psi Chapter of Kappa Kappa Psi at West Texas A&M University who are in good standing. Membership is available to brothers who graduated from WTAMU or departed the University in good standing with alumni status in accordance with the National Constitution.

Full membership is available to eligible members who are in good standing and maintain their required Kappa Kappa Psi national membership dues and Alpha Psi Alumni Association annual contribution.

Membership Dues

$15
Per year

Your annual contribution helps provide the foundation for our programs, scholarships, grants, service initiatives, and other opportunities supporting Alpha Psi.

  • Graduating SeniorsYour first year of local alumni membership is optional if you transitioned to alumni status within the preceding academic year.
  • Life MembersLife Members are exempt from annual local dues, but are always welcome to make a voluntary $15 contribution to support the Association.

Member Benefits

What does membership include?

As a member of AΨAA, you can:

  • Serve on the Alpha Psi Alumni Association Board of Directors
  • Attend alumni social and networking events
  • Connect and reconnect with Alpha Psi brothers
  • Participate in service opportunities
  • Engage in professional development opportunities
  • Receive our monthly newsletter
  • Receive Board of Directors meeting minutes and Association updates
  • Stay connected with news and updates from the active Alpha Psi Chapter
  • Support scholarships, grants, and programs benefiting our brothers and the WTAMU band programs
