How We’re Organized

The Board

The Board consists of four directors. Board members serve in a single position whenever possible. If a vacancy cannot be filled by another willing and available member, a current Board member may temporarily serve in an additional role.

  • PresidentPresides over meetings and keeps AΨAA in step with the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association and the active chapter.
  • Membership DirectorRecruits Alpha Psi alumni and oversees our awards, scholarships, and programs.
  • Communications DirectorRuns our social media and Discord, and shares meeting minutes with members.
  • Finance DirectorCollects dues, keeps the books, and reports on the Association’s finances.

Serving on the Board

Board Member Requirements

To serve on the Board of Directors, members must:

  • Be an Alumni, Honorary, and/or Life Member of the Alpha Psi Chapter of Kappa Kappa Psi in good standing.
  • Be a member of the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association.
  • Have the best interests of the Alpha Psi Alumni Association at heart.
  • Be willing and able to dedicate the time and effort necessary to fulfill the responsibilities of their position.

Board elections are held annually, and terms run July 1 through June 30. See the AΨAA Bylaws for details.
