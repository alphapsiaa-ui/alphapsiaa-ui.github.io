Who We Are

About Us

We are a home for the alumni, honorary, and life members of the Alpha Psi Chapter of Kappa Kappa Psi at West Texas A&M University.

Our Mission

Why we exist

The mission of the Alpha Psi Alumni Association is to foster collaboration and social connections among Alumni, Honorary, and Life Members of the Alpha Psi Chapter at West Texas A&M University. Our primary aim is to provide support and scholarships to the Alpha Psi Chapter and its affiliated band programs, benefiting the communities of Randall and Potter County. We are committed to upholding the principles outlined in the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association Constitution.

AΨAA Bylaws, Article 2.1

What We Do

Brotherhood doesn’t end at graduation

Our LAA fosters meaningful connections with the active brothers while creating opportunities for continued service, leadership, music, fellowship, and brotherhood.

Service

Giving back to Alpha Psi, the WTAMU band programs, and the communities of Randall and Potter County.

Support

Scholarships, grants, and programs that help the active chapter thrive.

Music

Championing the college band as a medium of musical expression, just as our founders did in 1947.

Fellowship

Reconnecting old friends and building new ones across every generation of the herd.

Our Place in the Fraternity

Part of something bigger

The Alpha Psi Alumni Association was established as a full organization in 2025 and is affiliated with the Kappa Kappa Psi Alumni Association. We abide by the rules and regulations of the KKΨAA Constitution.

We cooperate with the national alumni associations of Kappa Kappa Psi and Tau Beta Sigma, the Southwest District Alumni Association, and the other Local Alumni Associations across the Southwest District.
