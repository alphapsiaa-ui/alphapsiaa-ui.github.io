Welcome Home

Welcome to the Alpha Psi Alumni Association!

Founded on June 9, 2025, we are a home for the alumni, honorary, and life members of Alpha Psi at West Texas A&M University.

Our LAA fosters meaningful connections with the active brothers while creating opportunities for continued service, leadership, music, fellowship, and brotherhood. Together, we support the Alpha Psi Chapter and carry the spirit of brotherhood forward.
